## Overall Impression

Careful, honest interaction layer (settle, pay, undo) inside a composition that works against the job: the weekly visitor gets a big balance, insight card and pills first while the overdue R$ 8.107,37 statement sits under the bottom nav, and one red covers expenses, due totals, card debt, a budget meter that can never be green, and the real overdue bill. Biggest opportunity: order Today around "what needs me", then give red one meaning.

## What's Working

1. Settle: 44px ◯, optimistic update, undo toast that says what happened, PAID badge persists so the overdue block doesn't vanish; desktop gets labelled Settle + calendar (`entry-list.tsx:97-133`, `:440-476`).
2. Pay statement sheet: restates what it records, shows source balances with a "short on" warning, primary button carries the amount, Escape returns focus (`pay-statement-dialog.tsx:73-125`).
3. Numbers show their formula ("cash ÷ fixed cost", "income − expense − contributions", "so far · closes 14/09", `—` never 0%).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Today's first screen doesn't answer "what do I need to do right now"** (reopens the recorded "overdue below the fold" decision)
- What: at 360×800 the overdue Nubank Aug/26 statement starts at y≈683; its Pay (y 739–775) sits under the nav (top 744); Next 7 days is below the fold; keyboard reaches Pay on tab 13, first ◯ on tab 16. Closed statements moved into that slot after the decision; §7 asks for Overdue to be prominent.
- Why: the visit is about settling and paying; the first screen is status plus pills that duplicate the FAB.
- Fix: when anything is overdue/due, a compact "Needs you" block under the header (name, amount, Pay/◯); insight folded into one line under the hero; pills below the lists on mobile; calm layout when nothing is due.
- Command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Red means everything, and the budget maths guarantees it**
- What: `expenseCents / budgetCents` (`review/page.tsx:70`) with budget = sum of caps (`metrics.ts:180`), so uncapped spending (Moradia R$ 2.585, Saúde, Outros) counts against it → "R$ 6.260,06 of R$ 3.900,00 · 161%", all six bars red; capped categories alone are ~86% (at risk). "Due in 7 days" is red whenever > 0 (`page.tsx:134`), as are the Expense tile and Total card debt, against §8. "Within" bar colour is near-white `--chart-1` (`budget-bars.tsx:9`). Light-mode green fails contrast (3.7:1).
- Why: the red that should stop the owner (overdue statement) reads like a routine expense; a budget that can't be green is ignored.
- Fix: compare the budget with capped-category spend (or show uncapped as a grey segment); red only for overdue, over-cap, negative leftover; due/expense/debt in text colour with sign/arrow; real "within" colour; `emerald-700` in light mode.
- Command: /impeccable colorize (plus the small domain fix)

**[P1] Add: Save is out of reach and errors show where the eye isn't**
- What: Save top at y≈916 (below an 800px viewport; ~470px scroll with keyboard up); `FormError` at the top (`entry-form.tsx:155`); Account half-width beside an empty column; Installments/Repeat/Notes always visible; kind asked again after the sheet.
- Why: entering several expenses is half the weekly loop.
- Fix: hide the nav on `/add` and pin a full-width Save bar; scroll/focus the error; Account beside Description; "More options" disclosure; with `?kind=`, show the kind as a title.
- Command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Settling can happen by accident; "another day" is touch-only on phones**
- What: `onTouchMove` uses X only (`entry-list.tsx:369-376`), so a diagonal scroll can settle; Undo is top-centre for 6s (`app/layout.tsx:38`). Below md, settle-on-date is a 450ms touch hold (`:350-357`), the calendar button is `hidden md:flex` (`:472`). No `scroll-padding-bottom` for the fixed nav (only `scroll-padding-inline`, `globals.css:144`).
- Fix: axis-lock the gesture after ~10px; toast at bottom-center above the nav; calendar button at all widths or "Settle on…" in a row menu; `scroll-padding-bottom` = nav height.
- Command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Every entry row starts with a fake menu button**
- What: seed leaves category icon/colour null; `CategoryIcon` falls back to `MoreHorizontal` (`category-icon.tsx:60`), the More-tab glyph; grey "…" disc on every row, aria-hidden and inert, costing 36px ("Celular" → "Ce…" on Cards).
- Fix: initial-letter disc, per-kind glyph, or no icon; seed icons/colours.
- Command: /impeccable clarify
